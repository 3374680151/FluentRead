# Sentence structure

See the role of each phrase to make a long sentence easier to understand.

<GrammarDemo en />

## Use it on a webpage

1. Enable **Selection translation** and choose **Card view**.
2. Configure a service and model under **AI explanations**.
3. Select a sentence, open its translation card, and choose **Structure**.
4. Choose a phrase, or use the left and right arrow keys, to read its explanation.

The top line keeps the original word order. Labels below it show sentence roles and parts of speech. These differ: a noun can be a subject in one sentence and an object in another.

## When labels are unavailable

Your selected AI generates the analysis, so treat it as a learning reference. If its response cannot be aligned with the sentence or uses an unsupported format, the card keeps the text explanation. You can retry or choose another model.

Opening a card does not call AI. A deliberate learning action sends the selection and only the context you permitted to the configured service.
