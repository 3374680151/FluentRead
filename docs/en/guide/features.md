## Page translation

Read translations below the original or show translations only. [Open the guide](/en/guide/webpage-translation)

## Section translation

Choose Section in the menu, select an area, then confirm translation. [Open the guide](/en/guide/webpage-translation)

## Selection translation

Select text and click the nearby FluentRead icon to see its translation. [Open the guide](/en/guide/deepseek-harness)

## Hover translation

Point at a paragraph and press Control to translate or restore it. [Open the guide](/en/guide/hover-translation)

## Input translation

Write your text, then translate it with your chosen trigger. It does not send the message. [Open the guide](/en/guide/input-translation)

<span id="images-files-and-subtitles"></span>

## Images, files and captions

- [Images and manga](/en/guide/image-translation): read translations over an image or translate manga as you scroll.
- [Area translation](/en/guide/area-translation): select text drawn in screenshots, charts, or video frames.
- [Documents](/en/guide/document-translation): import, translate, edit, and download files.
- [Video and meeting captions](/en/guide/video-subtitles): compare captions supplied by supported platforms.

<span id="translation-center-compare-services"></span>

## Translation Center: compare providers

Compare several providers’ translations of the same text. [Open the guide](/en/guide/translation-center)

<span id="english-word-lookup"></span>

## English dictionary lookup

Read English dictionary definitions or listen in the selection card. [Open the guide](/en/guide/deepseek-harness)

<span id="ai-reading-card-and-learning-center"></span>

## Selection learning and Learning Center

Request an AI explanation when needed, or save words and sentences to find, listen to, and review in Learning Center. [Open the guide](/en/guide/vocabulary-book)

## Bilingual share cards

Turn an original, translation, and source into an image you can share. [Open the guide](/en/guide/share-cards)

## Translation style

Choose translation colors, size, and appearance. [Open the guide](/en/config/appearance)

## Bilingual sentence highlighting

Enable sentence highlighting, then point at a sentence to highlight its matching original or translation. [Open the guide](/en/config/appearance)

## Smart Highlighting

Mark keywords or words that are harder to predict in context to help scan the text. [Open the guide](/en/guide/information-highlight)

## Writing assistant

Draft or improve Gmail and GitHub replies, review and insert them, then send manually. [Open the guide](/en/guide/writing-assistant)

<span id="your-service-and-settings"></span>

## Multiple translation providers

Start with free translation or configure your own provider and model. [Open the guide](/en/config/translation-engines)

## Automatic translation and site rules

Set automatic translation, languages, or exceptions for a website. [Open the guide](/en/config/site-adaptation)

## Restore, styles and themes

Customize [reading and interface settings](/en/config/appearance), or [export a backup and restore settings on another device](/en/config/backup-sync).

## More translation languages

Choose source and target languages independently of the interface language. [Open the guide](/en/guide/webpage-translation)

### Request limits

If a provider reports too many requests, lower its concurrency or request rate in [request limits](/en/config/#request-limits).
